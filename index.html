Privacy Policy

Lakshadweep Tourism VR

Meta Quest immersive tourism experience

Effective date: November 9, 2026

About the experience. Lakshadweep Tourism VR introduces visitors to Lakshadweep through a history video, an interactive map for selecting islands, and immersive 360-degree island videos on Meta Quest. This policy explains information that may be handled when you use the experience.

1. Who we are

Exarplay, a brand of XR Horizon Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”), publishes Lakshadweep Tourism VR (“the App”). For questions about this Privacy Policy, please email xrexarplay@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the App

The described experience does not require you to create an in-app account or enter your name, email address, phone number, or postal address to explore the islands. To operate the VR experience, the App uses headset pose and controller inputs supplied by Meta Quest to determine what you look at, which island you select, and how you interact with video playback.

The history and island videos are displayed as content; they are not recordings of you. Features described for the App do not require microphone recording, eye-tracking access, or access to personal photos. If such features are introduced later, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

3. Media playback, network activity and device storage

Videos may be bundled with the installation or delivered from a remote media host, depending on the deployed version. If a video is streamed or downloaded, the hosting provider may receive technical connection data such as IP address, request time, and device or network details necessary to provide the content. Any applicable third-party host may handle that information under its own privacy terms.

The App or headset may retain temporary media caches or local preferences needed for playback or operation. You can generally remove the App's locally managed content by uninstalling it, subject to Meta Quest operating-system behavior.

4. How information is used

  • To display the historical introduction and 360-degree island experiences.
  • To respond to headset movement, controller input and map selections.
  • To load media and maintain the App's operation.
  • To respond to messages if you contact us for support.

5. Meta and other service providers

Meta provides the device operating system, app distribution and related platform services. Meta may process device, account and diagnostic information independently in accordance with its own privacy notices. You can review Meta's Privacy Center. If the App uses third-party content delivery, analytics, crash reporting or other SDKs, those services may process additional information; the policy and the store's data disclosures should be reviewed and updated to match the shipped build.

6. Advertising and sale of information

We do not sell personal information gathered through the described island-tour experience. We do not use the headset pose or island selections for behavioral advertising as part of the features described above.

7. Retention and deletion requests

The App is designed primarily to present media rather than maintain a separate user profile. Local files and caches are managed by the App and the headset. If you contact us for support, we may retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request and meet applicable obligations.

You can ask to access or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing xrexarplay@gmail.com with the subject “Lakshadweep Tourism VR – Privacy Request.” Please do not send sensitive information or account passwords. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for audiences permitted by its Meta Horizon Store age rating and Meta Quest account requirements. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the described experience. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns at the email above.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. International availability

Meta and any third-party media services may process technical information in locations outside your country. Their privacy notices describe their own data-handling practices.
